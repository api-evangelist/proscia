Digital pathology’s benefits to diagnostic laboratories aren’t limited to the routine clinical practice. The data-driven approach unlocks closer ties between clinical care and the breakthroughs in drug discovery and development that are advancing how we diagnose and treat disease. This synergy between diagnostic labs and life sciences organisations will help bring the broader vision of precision medicine within reach.
