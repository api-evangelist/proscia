We’re introducing Proscia Aperture to bridge the gap between laboratories and life sciences organizations and accelerate precision medicine. Unlike the retrospective datasets that pharma has traditionally relied on, Aperture delivers prospective, real-time insights at the moment of diagnosis. It empowers them to advance novel therapies with greater speed.
