Cytology has always been one of the most technically demanding areas of pathology. Unlike histology, where tissue sections are relatively uniform, cytology samples require pathologists. Concentriq makes cytology enterprise-ready, enabling customers to digitally review, collaborate on, and scale cytology workflows alongside histology.
